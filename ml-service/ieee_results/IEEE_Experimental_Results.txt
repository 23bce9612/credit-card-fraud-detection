7. EXPERIMENTAL ANALYSIS
==================================================

The experimental results show that all evaluated models exhibit different trade-offs between fraud detection and false-positive control.

Random Forest achieved the highest F1-score of 84.15% and precision of 90.59%, indicating a stronger balance between precision and recall among the evaluated models according to these reported metrics.

Logistic Regression achieved the highest recall of 91.84%, but its precision was 6.10%. AdaBoost achieved a recall of 89.80%, with a precision of 7.34%.

The proposed ensemble achieved a recall of 88.78%, precision of 60.84%, and F1-score of 72.20%. Its ROC-AUC was 0.9761, and its Average Precision was 0.8638.

These results illustrate the importance of considering multiple evaluation metrics when assessing fraud detection systems. Accuracy alone may not fully represent model effectiveness when fraudulent transactions form a small proportion of the dataset.

==================================================
8. CONCLUSION
==================================================

The evaluated FraudGuard ensemble model achieved 99.88% accuracy, 88.78% recall, and a ROC-AUC of 0.9761 on the reported test set. It correctly identified 87 out of 98 fraudulent transactions while producing 56 false-positive predictions.

Although the ensemble demonstrated strong recall and ROC-AUC, Random Forest achieved a higher F1-score and precision in this evaluation. Therefore, the models should be compared according to the intended operational priorities, including fraud detection, false alarms, and the cost of missed fraudulent transactions.
